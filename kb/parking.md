# Parking

## The Alden
The Alden includes one garage stall. A second stall is $175 a month. Guests park in the garage. Guest parking is $15 a day at the Cedar Street gate, paid at the lobby desk. Guests display the receipt on the dash. The garage gate closes if the receipt is missing, and the car can be towed after one warning.

## Birch Court
Birch Court includes one surface space. A second space is $75 a month. Guests use the marked visitor row for up to 12 hours and do not park in reserved stalls. Overnight guests need a pass from the desk.

## Harbor Lofts
Harbor Lofts has no assigned stall. Street parking is unmetered after 6:00 p.m. The bike room is included. One paid garage stall in the Wharf Lane garage is $220 a month and has a waitlist.
