# Packages

## All buildings
Packages are held for 48 hours. After that the carrier is asked to pick them up. The desk does not sign for cash, checks, or anything perishable. Mark the unit number on every order.

## The Alden
The Alden package room is off the lobby, next to the mailboxes. Lockers text a code when a parcel is scanned.

## Birch Court
Birch Court package shelves are in the laundry room of building A. Oversized boxes stay in the maintenance cage and the desk calls the unit.

## Harbor Lofts
Harbor Lofts packages are in the lobby cage. The freight elevator is not used for daily parcels.
