# Laundry

## The Alden
The Alden has a laundry room on level 2. Machines take the resident portal wallet, not coins. A wash is $2.25 and a dry is $1.75. Report a stuck machine in this chat and the desk refunds the cycle after the vendor confirms it.

## Birch Court
Birch Court laundry is in building A, next to the package shelves. Same prices as The Alden. The room locks at 10:00 p.m.

## Harbor Lofts
Harbor Lofts homes have in-unit laundry. The building has no shared laundry room. Washer hoses are the resident's responsibility if they leak from a loose connection.
