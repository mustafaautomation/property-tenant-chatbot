# Trash and recycling

## The Alden
The Alden trash chute is locked from 9:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. Bags must be tied. Do not leave furniture by the chute. Recycling bins are on level 2 next to the mail room: paper, glass, and plastic, rinsed.

## Birch Court
Birch Court dumpsters sit behind building B. Trash and recycling both go there. Blue bins are recycling. Break down boxes. Bulk items need a desk ticket and are picked up on Thursday.

## Harbor Lofts
Harbor Lofts has valet trash on Tuesday and Friday. Set tied bags inside the unit door before 9:00 a.m. Recycling is the blue tote in the kitchen, taken with the trash. The chute in the old warehouse is closed.
