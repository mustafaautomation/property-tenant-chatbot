# Guest policy

## All buildings
A guest may stay 14 nights in a row and no more than 30 nights in a calendar year. Longer stays need written desk approval and an added occupant on the lease. Guests use the resident's fob and do not get their own. The resident is responsible for guest noise, parking, and damage.

## The Alden
Overnight guests at The Alden register at the lobby desk after 9:00 p.m. so the night attendant expects them. Guest parking is paid separately.
