# Buildings

## The Alden
The Alden is 410 Cedar Street, Cedarhaven, an 18-story building with 210 homes. The resident desk in the lobby is open weekdays 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and Saturday 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Packages are held in the lobby room for 48 hours. The water shutoff is the utility closet at the end of each floor hall, labeled WATER.

## Birch Court
Birch Court is 88 Birch Court, Cedarhaven, 84 garden homes in three walk-up buildings. There is no elevator. The water shutoff is the mechanical closet under the stair in each building, labeled WATER. Package shelves are in the laundry room of building A.

## Harbor Lofts
Harbor Lofts is 15 Wharf Lane, Cedarhaven, 62 lofts in a converted warehouse. The freight elevator is for moves and is booked with the desk. The water shutoff is the basement meter room off the lobby, labeled WATER. The bike room is on the alley side.
