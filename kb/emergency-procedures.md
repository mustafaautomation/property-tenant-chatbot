# Emergency procedures

## All buildings
Call 911 first if anyone is in danger, then the Keystone emergency line at (555) 019-2400. Flooding, a gas odor, fire or smoke, no heat in winter, and a lockout are emergencies. They are not routine work orders. Do not wait for the next business day.

## Flooding
If water is pouring from a ceiling or a pipe has burst, leave if water is near outlets. Shut the valve labeled WATER only if you can do it safely. A sink that is dripping or a faucet that is leaking is a maintenance ticket, not this line, unless water is spreading through the unit.

## Gas
If you smell gas or rotten eggs, leave the building. Do not flip switches and do not use the elevator. Call 911 and (555) 019-2400 from outside. A gas stove that will not light, with no odor, is an appliance ticket.

## Fire
Leave by the stairs. Do not use the elevator. Pull the nearest alarm. Call 911, then the Keystone emergency line at (555) 019-2400. A smoke detector that is only chirping needs a battery and is a routine ticket. Visible smoke or flames is this procedure.

## No heat
No heat is an emergency when the outdoor temperature is under 40°F or the indoor temperature falls below 68°F. Do not use a gas stove or an unattended space heater as a substitute. The on-call technician brings temporary heat. No hot water, with the heat still working, is an urgent plumbing ticket.

## Lockout
Call (555) 019-2400. After-hours lockouts are $85, payable to the locksmith, and you need a photo ID. During desk hours a lockout at the Alden desk is free with photo ID.
