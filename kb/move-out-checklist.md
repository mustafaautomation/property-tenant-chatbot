# Move-out checklist

## All buildings
Schedule the move-out inspection at least 2 business days ahead. Weekends are not inspection days. The inspection covers walls, appliances, keys, and the parking fob. Remove all belongings, patch nail holes, and replace stove drip pans if they are burned through. Return every key and fob at the inspection. Utilities should stay on through the inspection date. The deposit is mailed within 21 days of the inspection, minus documented charges.
