# Maintenance entry

## All buildings
The desk gives at least 24 hours' notice for non-emergency entry, between 9:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. on weekdays. Emergencies (flooding, gas, fire, no heat, lockout) can be entered without notice. If the resident grants permission, the technician may enter when no one is home. Pets must be crated or out of the room.
