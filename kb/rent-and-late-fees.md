# Rent and late fees

## All buildings
Rent is due on the 1st. A payment received through the 5th is on time. On the 6th a $75 late fee posts. Starting on the 11th, $10 is added each day. Late fees cap at $200 per month. Pay by ACH in the resident portal, by debit card in the portal (a 2.5% convenience fee applies), or by check at the Alden desk. Cash over $50 is not accepted. The portal posts the same day before 8:00 p.m. Central. A check is posted on the business day it is received.
