# Pet policy

## The Alden
The Alden allows one cat. Dogs are not housed, including visiting dogs. The cat fee is $400 and cat rent is $35 a month. The cat must be registered before move-in, and the unit needs a scratching post so the door casings stay intact.

## Birch Court
Birch Court allows up to two pets. Dogs must be under 40 pounds at maturity, and breeds on the city's restricted-breed list are not housed. The pet fee is $500 and pet rent is $45 a month. Dogs are leashed in the courtyard and are not tied to the grill.

## Harbor Lofts
Harbor Lofts does not house pets. An assistance animal needs documentation at least 14 days before move-in. Visiting animals are not allowed, including for a weekend.
