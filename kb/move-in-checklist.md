# Move-in checklist

## All buildings
Before keys are released, the lease is signed, renter's insurance is on file, and the first month plus any pet fee is paid. On move-in day, walk the unit with the condition form and photograph existing marks. Test the stove, the refrigerator, the sinks, and the heat or air. Register pets and vehicles the same day. Fobs are live at 1:00 p.m. on the lease start date, not before. Deliveries are held at the package room for 48 hours.
