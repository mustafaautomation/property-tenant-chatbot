# Amenities

## All buildings
Bookings open 7 days ahead. Each reservation is a 2-hour slot. A household may hold one upcoming reservation. Cancel at least 24 hours ahead or the next booking request waits 14 days. Quiet hours still apply, and amplified music is not allowed. The resident who booked the room stays on site.

## The Alden
The Alden rooftop lounge and the party room are booked in this chat. Both close at 8:00 p.m. The Alden gym is first come, first served and is not reserved. Gym hours are 5:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.

## Birch Court
The courtyard grill and the clubhouse are at Birch Court. The grill is bookable from 11:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Extinguish coals before you leave. The clubhouse closes at 9:00 p.m.

## Harbor Lofts
The Harbor Lofts roof deck is bookable until 8:00 p.m. Wind can close the deck. A sign in the lobby is the notice, and a booking is refunded as a cancellation when the deck is closed.
